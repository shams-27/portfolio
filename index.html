About me

Hello! I am a passionate Software Engineering undergraduate at Daffodil International University, driven by a deep curiosity for how things work under the hood. My programming journey stems from a strong analytical background in science and mathematics, which naturally guided me toward system architecture, logic, and problem-solving.

Currently, I am heavily focused on mastering Computer Science fundamentals—specifically Data Structures and Algorithms (DSA), Object-Oriented Programming (OOP), and Operating Systems (OS).
